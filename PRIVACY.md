# Joy2Cloud privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Joy2Cloud does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension handles, and where it stays

- **Controller input.** Button, stick and battery data from your Joy-Con is
  received over Bluetooth by the Joy2Cloud tab in your browser and passed to
  the cloud gaming tab you have open, inside the same browser. It is never
  sent anywhere else and is not recorded.
- **Settings.** Your language, theme, button layout, vibration preferences
  and stick calibration are saved with `chrome.storage.local`, on your own
  device. They are not synced or uploaded.
- **Websites.** The extension runs only on the cloud gaming and gamepad
  testing sites listed in its manifest, and only to present the Joy-Con as a
  game controller to those pages and to pass vibration back to the
  controllers. It does not read, change or collect anything else on those
  pages, and it does not run on any other site.

Joy2Cloud makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no tracking,
no remote code, no accounts.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also clear
the stick calibration from the Diagnostics section of the Joy2Cloud tab.
